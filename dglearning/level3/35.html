35. Format the axes of trends chart.

  1. Open trends.dg5
    1. Right click on body – chart, select Edit Symbol
      1. Click on trendsGraph – Chart- vAxes – AxisRenderer
        1. Axis Properties
          1. Font Color: Bind from Project Dataflow – fontColor
          2. Labels: Auto
          3. Label Gap: 10
          4. Advanced, Major Ticks: None
          5. Advanced, Minor Ticks: None
          6. Base at Zero: false
      2. Click on trendsGraph – Chart- hAxes – AxisRenderer
        1. Axis Properties
          1. Font Color: Bind from Project Dataflow – fontColor
          2. Labels: Auto
          3. Label Gap: 10
          4. Line Stroke: None
          5. Line Stroke Width: 0px
          6. Advanced, Major Ticks: None
          7. Advanced, Minor Ticks: None
      3. Click on trendsGraph – Chart – Series – Series – Master Series
        1. Master Series Properties
          1. X Original Column: “timestamp”
          2. Y Original Column: “v0”
      4. Click on trendsGraph – Chart – Series
        1. Repeater: Bind from metricGraph – Chart dataflow Multi-Histories - output
    2. Save Symbol
  2. Save page
